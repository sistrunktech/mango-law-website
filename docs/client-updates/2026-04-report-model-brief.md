## Objective

Write a client-facing monthly report for Mango Law that is:

- non-technical
- honest
- useful to a law-firm owner
- focused on progress, business value, and next steps

This report is for the Sistrunk Tech reporting workflow. It is not content for the Mango Law website.

- Emphasize that the site is healthier, clearer, and more aligned to OVI/criminal-defense priorities than it was before.

## Things to include

- short executive summary
- what was completed
- why the completed work matters to Mango Law as a business
- a simple performance snapshot
- local listing / citation cleanup progress
- what still needs work
- next-month priorities
- any client asks or dependencies

## Things to avoid

- do not mention branch names, pull requests, commits, worktrees, or deployment mechanics
- do not use jargon like `URL Inspection`, `origin/main`, `SSR`, `SSG`, `crawl bucket`, or `Citation Builder lag`
- do not overclaim a broad ranking recovery
- do not claim new backlinks, directory wins, or local-pack gains unless they are clearly verified
- do not make the report read like an engineering changelog
